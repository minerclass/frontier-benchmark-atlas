Contributing

Adding a harness

  1. Give the harness the brief in PROMPT.md unchanged.
  2. It writes index.html, data.json (format in SCHEMA.md) and COMPILATION.md under harnesses/<id>/.
  3. It adds or updates its entry in harnesses/manifest.json.
  4. python tools/validate.py checks the format and lists disagreements with other harnesses.
