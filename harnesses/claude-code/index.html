Frontier Benchmark Atlas

Between 1 September and 13 October 2026, Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, SpaceXAI and Meta each shipped a frontier model alongside a launch table in which that model mostly wins. This atlas places those tables beside independent runs of the same tests. Where the two agree, the claim holds. Where they part, the gap usually traces back to effort settings, test harnesses, or benchmark versions.

Method

Sources and limits

  • A claim is a figure a lab published about its own model. A rival table figure is one a lab published about another lab's model. Independent means an Artificial Analysis run, including runs that Meta's model card reproduces.
  • Primary pages were read for Anthropic's four launches, Google's Argon post, Cursor's Grok 4.7 page and the Artificial Analysis leaderboard. OpenAI's launch pages and Meta's model card could not be retrieved directly; their figures come from the reports listed below and from rival tables that cite them. Tooltips name the source for each figure.
  • Google's comparison table comes from Emergent's reproduction of Google's evaluation methodology document. Google's announcement post itself contains no table.
  • Index scores are Intelligence Index v4.3.x. Launch-day figures under v4.1.x, such as Fable 5.1 at 65.7 and GPT-6 Astra at 61.2, are not comparable with them.
  • Gemini 4 Argon: trackers date the announcement and a limited rollout to 30 September. Google's post describing API and app availability is dated 13 October.
  • All numbers are as published by 13 October 2026 and will move as evaluators rerun models.
