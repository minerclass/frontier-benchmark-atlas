Frontier Benchmark Atlas

Between 1 September and 12 November 2026, Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, SpaceXAI and Meta shipped fifteen models, most with a launch table. In seven of the nine tables collected here, the publisher's model leads most rows where a rival lab appears. This atlas places those tables beside independent runs of the same tests. Where the two agree, the claim holds. Where they part, the gap usually traces back to effort settings, test harnesses, or benchmark versions.

Independent runs land below most claims. On Terminal-Bench 4.0, Artificial Analysis measured Grok 4.7 at 25.8% against SpaceXAI's 37.6% at the same xHigh label. GPT-6.1 Terra goes the other way: 55.6% measured against OpenAI's 50.3%.

Method

Sources and limits

  • A claim is a figure a lab published about its own model. A rival table figure is one a lab published about another lab's model. Independent means an Artificial Analysis run, including runs that Meta's model card reproduces.
  • Primary pages were read for Anthropic's five launches, OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra page and GPT-6.1 Terra post, Google DeepMind's Argon, Carbon and Hydrogen model cards and methodology PDFs, Meta's Muse Wonder and Muse Spark Open model cards and Muse Ember post, Cursor's Grok 4.7 page and board, and the Artificial Analysis leaderboard. The chart values in OpenAI's GPT-6.1 Sol and Terra posts are not transcribed; Sol's still come from Vellum and DataCamp, and Terra's only figure here is the 50.3% Terminal-Bench 4.0 score stated in the text. Tooltips name the source for each figure.
  • Google's announcement posts show their tables as images or not at all; the model cards print them as text. Emergent's earlier reproduction of the Argon table matches the card row for row.
  • Index scores are Intelligence Index v5.0.1, read 12 November. Artificial Analysis replaced v4.3 with v5 on 27 October, so the v4.3 figures in revisions 1 to 3 and launch-day figures under v4.1.x, such as Fable 5.1 at 65.7, are not comparable with them. Google's Carbon card and AA's Carbon article print v5.0 values, a point lower for some models (Carbon 59, Opus 5.5 58).
  • Claude Fable 5.1 and Muse Spark 1.3 are listed as deprecated on the Artificial Analysis leaderboard and have no v5 score, so they are out of the index charts.
  • Gemini 4 Argon: trackers date the announcement and a limited rollout to 30 September. Google's post describing API and app availability is dated 13 October. Gemini 4 Carbon: announced 28 October for selected cyber defenders and Fairwind Program partners; Google opened the paid API tier to developers on 10 November.
  • All numbers are as published by 12 November 2026 and will move as evaluators rerun models.
  • Revision 2, 13 October 2026: GPT-6 Astra's own Terminal-Bench 4.0 figure corrected from 57.7 to 57.9, and the CursorBench chart rebuilt from Cursor's public board, which adds Gemini 4 Argon, Claude Haiku 5.5, Muse Spark 1.3 and Composer 2.5. Both errors surfaced when this compilation was compared with Cursor's.
  • Revision 3, 26 October 2026 (Monday check-in): Muse Spark Open added (15 October). OpenAI's table was rebuilt from OpenAI's own page; the earlier reproduction had put Claude Fable 5's Agents' Last Exam score under Fable 5.1, repeated Fable 5.1's FrontierMath score for Fable 5, and printed different HealthBench figures for Opus 5 and Fable 5.1. OpenAI prints 52.6 for Opus 5 on Terminal-Bench 4.0, which settles the open question from revision 2. Google and Meta tables now cite their own cards, which match. CursorBench adds Muse Wonder; Artificial Analysis adds Terminal-Bench runs for Haiku 5.5 and Grok 4.7.
  • Revision 4, 12 November 2026: adds GPT-6.1 Terra (26 October, released the day revision 3 was checked but missed by it), Gemini 4 Carbon (28 October), Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 (9 November), Muse Ember (9 November) and Gemini 4 Hydrogen (12 November). New launch tables for Carbon, Hydrogen, Fable 5.2 and Ember, transcribed from Google DeepMind's model cards and Anthropic's and Meta's posts. The independent index, cost ladders and speeds are re-read on Intelligence Index v5.0.1, which replaces v4.3. CursorBench adds Carbon, Fable 5.2 and Hydrogen.
